Welcome to Weather Maps

This site hosts interactive 3D weather visualizations generated with Plotly from WRF (Weather Research and Forecasting) model and ML downscaled output for Rocky Mountain National Park.

Maps show 10 m wind and 2 m temperature from a machine learning model downscaling a 1 km grid-spacing WRF run to 333 m. Precipitation contours are from the 1 km model. Use the controls inside each map to play, pause, and scrub through time.

Select a map from the navigation bar above to get started. Maps take 5-10 seconds to load all frames and allow scrolling

Mouse: drag to orbit · Ctrl+drag to pan · scroll to zoom slowly.
Touch / D-pad: use the ✥ Pan / ↻ Orbit toggle and D-pad buttons in the nav bar, or swipe to the top right of the map window to access the Plotly controls menu and toggle touch and drag commands between pan and rotate.
